Mathematicians Should Read Rick Rubin

2026

I think mathematicians should read Rick Rubin. His ideas in The Creative Act and The Way of Code feel relevant to how we do mathematics, especially now that we’re working with AI. There’s something about being open to what comes, and letting go of how you think the work should happen, that I find useful.

We can get attached to a particular approach or to what we think an answer should look like. With AI, there’s an opportunity to let it try things we wouldn’t have tried ourselves. We still need to check and understand the mathematics, but we don’t have to decide every step in advance.

I think the harder part is how much of our identity is tied to doing the work ourselves. If a machine can do something we spent years learning to do, what does that mean for us? I don’t have a clear answer, but I think some of Rubin’s ideas can help. We can give ourselves room to explore what this makes possible, even if we’re still figuring out how we feel about it.
